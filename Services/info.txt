Här ska logiken för att läsa JSON-filen ligga.

Razor-sidorna ska inte själva öppna och läsa filen. I stället ska de be en service om informationen.
Razor-sida -> TopicService -> topic.JSON

Detta gör att varje del får ett tydligt ansvar:

Razor-sidan visar information.
Servicen hämtar information.
JSON-filen lagrar information.
Modellerna beskriver informationens struktur.


What TopicService should include (summary)

• Read Data\topics.json (use IWebHostEnvironment.ContentRootPath to locate file).
• Deserialize with System.Text.Json (PropertyNameCaseInsensitive = true).
• Async methods: GetTopicsAsync and GetTopicByIdAsync.
• Be thread-safe and stateless so it can be registered as Singleton (or Scoped).


• ITopicService defines a contract (what methods exist). TopicService implements the contract (how data is read). Razor components inject ITopicService and call its async methods during lifecycle (e.g., OnInitializedAsync) — separation of UI and data access.
